Why Your Boby Needs Exercise

Your boby is like a supercar the more you use it, the stronger it gets! kids who move for 60 minutes everyday are happier, sleep better, and do better at school.

Dacing, Swimming, football, yoga anything that gets your heart beating faster count. Keep moving and have fun!

Quick Health Tips

  • Eat 5 fruit and veggies every day
  • Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • Sleep 9 to 11 hours every night
  • Move your boby for 60 minutes
